01What I look for

Eight lenses I apply to an early-stage company. Hover a line to open it.

  • 01

    Market

    Size · Structure · Timing

    How large the opportunity really is, how it is structured, and why now.

  • 02

    Founder

    Team · Insight · Execution

    What the team knows that others don't — tested through structured founder interviews.

  • 03

    Product

    Wedge · Pull · Fit

    The company's wedge into the market and whether customers genuinely pull for it.

  • 04

    Business model

    Revenue · Pricing · Scale

    How the company earns, and whether that model strengthens as it grows.

  • 05

    Unit economics

    Margins · Payback · Durability

    Whether the economics can support a durable business, not just a fundraise.

  • 06

    Competition

    Map · Whitespace · Moat

    Where the company sits on the market map and what keeps others out.

  • 07

    Capital

    Rounds · Themes · Europe

    Tracking European funding rounds and the themes capital is moving toward.

  • 08

    Thesis

    Memo · Conviction · Risk

    Turning qualitative and quantitative diligence into a written investment memo.
